# G4X — Instrument ledger detail

Date: 2026-10-23. Baseline `5513c64`. Scope: one revision-consistent local ledger detail, independent of candle availability.

## Contract

- `GET /v1/portfolio/instrument-ledger` accepts only exact `instrument_id` and uppercase `currency`, once each. The ID preserves the snapshot opaque-ID contract. Symbol is a display label, not identity.
- One read-only transaction replays current FIFO holdings, proves the full canonical event log and reads the latest 50 original BUY/SELL records for that instrument/currency at the proved revision. All imported accounts are aggregated. A 51st row sets `has_more`; no total/completeness claim is made. No trades means 404, including dividend-only instruments; a previously traded closed position has zero quantity/cost and null average.
- Quantity and remaining cost stay exact. Only the display average uses existing exact rational division and half-even quantization to eight decimals; tiny positive averages may round to zero. Buy fees and ledger splits are reflected in remaining cost/quantity, while each trade retains its original price/quantity/fee/net cash.
- `ledger_as_of` is the latest occurrence in the whole revision. `ledger_recorded_at` belongs to the last appended event. Duplicate-only imports may advance metadata without an event; a clock correction may make an earlier event's recorded time later. Revision and canonical proof establish consistency, not wall-clock equality or monotonicity.
- The response excludes account, receipt, source-event and internal event identifiers. It is `local_ledger` and broker freshness is unverified. No write/schema/backup/import-mapping/broker authority changes.
- The existing shared Flutter activity parser checks exact BUY/SELL cash arithmetic with integer-scaled decimals. SELL proceeds may be zero or negative after fees; sign alone is not an integrity check. OpenAPI uses the same canonical Decimal contract.
- Holdings → asset detail → the always-available ledger action opens a separate page even when candles fail. Loading, initial retry, refresh failure retention, closed holdings, bound disclosure and single-announcement trade semantics use the existing design system. Requests are matched by ID/currency; the whole previous result and timestamps survive a failed refresh.

Candle time membership and price adjustment remain unspecified, so no average-cost line or trade marker is enabled.
